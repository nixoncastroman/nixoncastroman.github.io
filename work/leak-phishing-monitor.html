Case study 02 · Capstone with Bank Jatim

Leak & Phishing Early Detection

A monitor that searches public sites and the dark web for leaked Bank Jatim data and fake Bank Jatim sites, so the security team doesn’t have to do it by hand.

Role
Team lead and project manager, team of 5
Client
Bank Jatim, CISO division
When
Sep – Dec 2026 (ongoing)
Stack
FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React, Docker

8sources, checked every 6 hours

233items found so far

~15%false positives (target: under 20%)

How it works

  • Finds the bank’s subdomains from public records (certificate logs, HackerTarget, the Wayback Machine) to know what’s official.
  • Every 6 hours, searches 8 sources for the bank’s name: search engines, social media, paste sites, forums, certificate logs, phishing feeds, urlscan.io and the Tor index.
  • Labels each finding, for example a fake site, leaked data or brand abuse, and saves the source link and a hash as evidence. Look-alike domains are caught with typosquat, homoglyph and IDN checks.
  • Analysts review findings in a dashboard. A later scan never overwrites their decision.

It only looks at public sources. It never scans the bank’s systems or touches customer data.

My part

I lead the team and run the project with the bank’s CISO division: requirements, sprint planning and reviews. I also worked on the dashboard’s review flow, and switched the output from High/Medium/Low risk scores to labels with evidence, so analysts can see why something was flagged.

Accuracy

We checked 100 of the 233 findings by hand. The system got 15 wrong, a false-positive rate of about 15%. The bank’s target is under 20%.

The project ends in December 2026. Real findings stay with the bank.
